# CCM

Build a modern, professional, Christ-centered Cross-Cultural Mission Management web application.

The purpose of this application is to help churches, mission organizations, leaders, and supporters easily monitor, organize, pray for, and support church planter pastors serving in different mission fields.

The application should work beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with a clean, modern, minimal, and inspiring Christian design.

Design Theme:

- Modern UI

- White background

- Blue and green accent colors

- Warm mission photography

- Rounded cards

- Soft shadows

- Fast navigation

- Responsive layout

- Professional dashboard

- Easy to use even for older pastors

- Optimized for low internet connections

OVERALL GOAL

------------------------------------------------

The application should feel like a premium mission management platform that inspires churches to pray, give, and participate in the Great Commission.

Every screen should communicate professionalism, excellence, compassion, stewardship, and the mission of making disciples of all nations.

The overall experience should be clean, inspiring, highly organized, and intuitive for users of all ages.

Additional recommendations

To make the app even more valuable over time, I'd add these features:

Mission Dashboard that immediately answers: Where are our missionaries? How are they doing? What do they need?

Interactive mission map showing every church planter by location.

Prayer mode, where users can swipe through one missionary at a time with their photo, family, latest update, and prayer requests.

Support timeline so supporters can see ministry growth from commissioning to church multiplication.

Advanced filtering by region, province, people group, ministry type, and mission stage.

Annual ministry analytics with automatically generated reports and presentation slides.

Secure document storage for commissioning papers, training certificates, and ministry resources.

AI-powered summaries that turn monthly reports into concise updates for church newsletters or mission presentations.

Suggested information architecture

Instead of organizing only by Batch, use multiple dimensions so users can find missionaries quickly:

Geography: Country → Region → Province → City/Municipality → Mission Field

Mission Journey: Candidate → Training → Internship → Commissioned → Church Planting → Multiplication → Regional Leadership → Retired

Ministry Type: Church Planting, Campus Ministry, Children's Ministry, Tribal Ministry, Urban Ministry, Cross-Cultural Ministry

Status: Active, On Leave, Retired, Transferred, Completed

Sending Church or Organization: Helpful when multiple churches partner in missions.
